How to read this chart ＋

Bottom bars: volume by candle. Taller bars show more shares traded in the selected day, week, or month. Weekly candles group Monday-start weeks; monthly candles group calendar months. Periods at the edges may be partial. Green means the close was at or above the open; red means it was below. They do not measure buying versus selling.

Left bars: estimated volume by price. Each day's volume is distributed uniformly from its low to its high, weighted by overlap with each price bin. The profile recalculates as you zoom or pan. It is an approximation, not transaction-level volume.

Gold line: point of control. The center of the highest-volume estimated bin. This is a historical reference, not a predicted support level. Prices use the provider's split-adjusted OHLC without dividend adjustment; the newest daily bar may still be forming.
